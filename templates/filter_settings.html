Text Filter Options
Blacklist Keywords

Messages containing any of these keywords will be completely skipped and not forwarded. This is useful for filtering out ads, announcements, or specific content you don't want to forward.

Text Replacements

Simple word or phrase replacement. Every occurrence of the original text will be replaced with the replacement text. Case-sensitive.

Regular Expressions

Advanced pattern matching and replacement. For users familiar with regular expressions.

Tip

To remove text without replacing it, use an empty replacement value (e.g., text_to_remove:)

Note

Regular expressions are applied after simple text replacements.
